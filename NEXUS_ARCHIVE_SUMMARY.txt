THE NEXUS ARCHIVE — What Is It?
================================

The Nexus Archive is an interactive research platform for exploring
alternative history, ancient mysteries, and hidden connections in history.

Think of it like Wikipedia meets a research conspiracy board — but
organized, sourced, and community-driven. Every theory and claim on
the site has to be backed by real cited sources.


WHAT YOU CAN DO ON IT
─────────────────────

Explore a Knowledge Graph
  An interactive visual map where theories and historical events are
  connected by relationships. You can zoom in, click topics, and follow
  connections between things like Göbekli Tepe, the Great Pyramid,
  Atlantis, UAP programs, ancient texts, lost civilizations, and more.

Rabbit Hole Mode
  Click any topic and let the site guide you deeper and deeper through
  connected theories, one step at a time. It tracks how deep you've gone
  and shows you the path you took.

Multiple Views
  You can explore the same data as a 2D graph, a 3D universe/orbital
  view, a geographic globe, or a historical timeline.

Deep Research on Every Topic
  Each topic has: key claims, criticisms, the mainstream perspective,
  open questions, and linked academic sources with credibility ratings.

Submit Your Own Theories
  Signed-in users can propose new topics, connections, and sources.
  Everything goes through a review process before it's published.

AI Research Review
  Admins can run an AI analysis on any topic. It rates the evidence,
  flags hallucination risks, checks academic consensus, and recommends
  improvements.

Earn Ranks and Badges
  The more quality contributions you make, the higher your rank. You
  start as a "New Seeker" and can work your way up to "Archive Fellow"
  and beyond. 19 badges to earn along the way.


THE TRUST SYSTEM
────────────────

Every user has a trust score (0–100).
  - Get your submissions approved → score goes up
  - Submit fake or low-quality content → score drops
  - Banned users can't submit anything
  - You need a minimum trust score just to submit content

This keeps the archive clean and research-quality.


WHO IS IT FOR?
──────────────

Anyone curious about:
  - Ancient civilizations and lost history
  - Archaeological anomalies
  - UFOs and UAP research
  - Religious texts and mythology
  - Alternative archaeology and forbidden history
  - Hidden connections between historical events


IN ONE SENTENCE
───────────────

The Nexus Archive is a community-built, source-backed research platform
where you can explore and contribute to a living knowledge graph of
alternative history — and the deeper you dig, the more it rewards you.
